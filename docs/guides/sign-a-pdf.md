# How to sign a PDF

To sign a PDF, open [Sign PDF](https://lathe.ashwinsathian.com/sign-pdf), add your file, then draw, type or upload a signature and place it on the page. Your signature and document stay on your device and are not saved.

## Steps

1. Add your PDF.
2. Choose **Draw**, **Type** or **Upload**. An uploaded signature has its white background removed.
3. Click the page where it should go, or use **Place signature on page N**.
4. Drag the signature into position and resize it from the corner. With a keyboard, use the arrow keys, and Shift with the arrow keys to resize.
5. Select **Sign and save**, then **Check the result**.

## What kind of signature this is

Sign PDF places **an image of your signature**. It is not a certificate-based digital signature, so it does not prove who signed or detect later changes. Many everyday agreements accept a visual signature, but some contracts, government forms and regulated documents require a qualified or certificate-based one. Ask the other party or a lawyer if you are unsure.

## If the PDF is already digitally signed

Adding anything changes the file, which invalidates the existing signature. Lathe warns you when you open such a file.
