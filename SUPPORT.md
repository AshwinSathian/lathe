# Getting help

- Something broke: open a [bug report](https://github.com/AshwinSathian/lathe/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). Please do not attach private files.
- You want a tool: open a [tool request](https://github.com/AshwinSathian/lathe/issues/new?template=tool_request.yml).
- You have a question: start a [discussion](https://github.com/AshwinSathian/lathe/discussions).
- You found a vulnerability: follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md).

Lathe is maintained by one person in their spare time, so replies can take a while.
